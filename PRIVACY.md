## What this app is

CoreFit MD — a five-minute video-guided morning core routine. Clip-accurate playback (every set opens at 0:30), six-week progression, streaks, trilingual UI. Static,…

## What it collects

**Nothing.** No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no fingerprinting, no advertising identifier and
no email capture.

Its own files are local. **One feature reaches a third party, and only if you turn it on**: the video source switch offers *Local* (the clip shipped with the app) or *YouTube*. Choose YouTube and the video is embedded from `youtube-nocookie.com`, which then sees your IP address and sets whatever Google sets — under Google's terms, not these. The Local source sends nothing.

Your routine, streaks and settings never leave the device either way.

## What is stored on your device

Whatever the app remembers between visits stays on this device. Clearing the app's stored data erases it.

## Links that leave the app

Documentation and support pages contain ordinary links. Opening one takes you to that site, with its own
privacy policy; the app loads nothing from it.

## Erasing

Clear the app's stored data, or delete the app itself. Nothing is held anywhere else, so there is no other
copy to ask us to remove.

## Changes

If a future version ever adds a request that carries your data, this file changes in the same commit.
